Electrical Machine Research

Analysis of a Novel Flux Switching Transverse Flux Permanent Magnet Linear Motor

The novel flux switching transverse flux permanent magnet linear motor is proposed. With decoupling characteristics of magnetic load and electric load, the proposed motor is suitable for aerospace, missile launch and other fields, benefiting from high thrust force and low cost.

Development and Analysis of Novel Flux-Switching Transverse-Flux Permanent Magnet Linear Machine

The FSTFPMLM combines the advantages of transverse-flux and flux-switching linear machines. The work presents structure, operating principle, 3-D finite-element analysis, thrust characteristics, parameter influence, prototype development, and experimental validation.

Electromagnetic Characteristic of a Novel Linear Flux Switching Machine With Three-Dimensional Magnetic Circuit

A linear flux switching permanent magnet machine with a three-dimensional magnetic circuit is introduced for applications needing high thrust force and low cost. The page includes equivalent magnetic circuit analysis, finite-element modelling, and prototype results.

The Thrust Characteristics of a Novel Transverse Flux Permanent Magnet Linear Motor with the Concentrated Flux Mover

This work studies a transverse flux permanent magnet linear motor with concentrated flux of three side walls in the mover, focusing on high force density, fault-tolerant operation, thrust force, cogging force, and force ripple.

Development and Analysis of a Novel Transverse Flux Permanent Magnet Linear Motor With the Concentrated Flux Mover

The original page lists this as another electrical machine research item connected to magnetic field computation and performance characteristics of the concentrated-flux mover topology.
